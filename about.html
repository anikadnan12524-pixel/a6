About The Clothing Hub

A free, independent reference for everyday clothes: what they’re made of, how they should fit and how to look after them.

Our story

Why a clothing hub?

Buying and caring for clothes involves a surprising amount of know-how: confusing size labels, unfamiliar fabric names and care symbols nobody explains. The Clothing Hub brings that knowledge together in one clear, easy-to-use place.

We publish plain-English guides to fabrics, sizing and garment care, and build free tools for converting sizes, choosing fabrics and editing your wardrobe. Our guides are researched using textile references and care-labelling information.

The Clothing Hub is independent. We don’t sell clothes and aren’t paid to recommend brands or retailers.
