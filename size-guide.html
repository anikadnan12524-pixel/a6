The size guide

How to measure yourself accurately and how clothing and shoe sizes compare across the US, UK, Europe, Japan and Australia.

Step one

How to measure yourself

  1. Chest or bust: around the fullest part, under the arms, keeping the tape level.
  2. Waist: around your natural waist, the narrowest part above the belly button.
  3. Hips: around the fullest part of the hips and seat, feet together.
  4. Inside leg: from the top of the inner thigh to the ankle bone.
  5. Sleeve: from the centre back of the neck, over the shoulder, to the wrist.
  6. Feet: stand on paper, mark heel and longest toe and measure in the afternoon.

Shoes

Shoe size tips

  • Measure both feet and use the larger one.
  • Leave about a thumb’s width (1 cm) beyond your longest toe.
  • European sizes don’t split into halves in most brands; size up if between sizes.
  • Use the size converter for quick US, UK and EU comparisons.
